# Taxonomy data attribution

`onet-acronyms.json` includes information from the O*NET 31.0 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and
Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
O*NET is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. This project has modified the information (only acronym and expansion pairs derived by
`backend/jobs/tools/taxonomy/derive_onet_acronyms.py`). USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.

ESCO (European Commission) was also considered: its terms allow reuse with attribution, but the download needs a form and
its API was unreachable from the build environment, so no ESCO data is used.
